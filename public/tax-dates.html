Tax dates

Key dates for your 2026 taxes

The deadlines that matter most for individuals, self-employed people and small corporations. Missing a date can mean interest and penalties, so put these in your calendar.

Individuals and self-employed

DateWhat's due
March 1, 2027Last day to contribute to your RRSP and deduct it on your 2026 return.
March 15, 2027First quarterly tax instalment for 2027, if the CRA has asked you to pay by instalments.
April 30, 2027Personal tax filing deadline for most people. Any balance owing for 2026 is due today, including for self-employed people.
June 15, 2027Filing deadline for self-employed individuals and their spouses or common-law partners. Also the second quarterly instalment.
September 15, 2027Third quarterly tax instalment.
December 15, 2027Fourth quarterly tax instalment.

Corporations

DeadlineWhat's due
2 or 3 months after year-endBalance of corporate tax owing. Many small Canadian-controlled private corporations have 3 months; most other corporations have 2.
6 months after year-endT2 corporate income tax return. For a December 31, 2026 year-end, that's June 30, 2027.

These dates are general information for the 2026 tax year. Your own deadlines can differ depending on your situation. Confirm with the Canada Revenue Agency or contact us.
