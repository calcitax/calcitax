BC Hydro now offering up to five free smart thermostats for baseboard-heated homes

By Calcitax Advisors · November 19, 2026

If your home is heated with electric baseboards, BC Hydro will now send you up to five smart thermostats for free, with free shipping. The catch is small: you join BC Hydro's Peak Saver program, which can turn your heat down by a degree or so for a few hours on the coldest, busiest days, and you can earn a $50 bill credit for taking part.

What happened

The Province announced the offer on July 6, 2026, as part of BC Hydro's Power Smart 2.0 plan, which it describes as a $1-billion, three-year investment in saving electricity. The thermostats come from two Canadian makers, Mysa and Sinopé. Ordering has now opened for the fall, and BC Hydro's offer page links straight to both companies' order pages. BC Hydro says the set of up to five thermostats is valued at over $600.

A smart thermostat is a wall control you can program and adjust from a phone app. It lets you lower the heat while you sleep or are out, which is where most of the savings come from.

Who qualifies

  • You have a residential BC Hydro account and an online MyHydro account.
  • Your home is heated with electric baseboard heaters. Homes heated with a heat pump don't qualify.
  • The thermostats are installed at the address on the BC Hydro account used to order them.
  • You enrol in Peak Saver when you check out. You can't get the free thermostats without it.
  • Renters can take part, but BC Hydro says you need your landlord's permission first.

How Peak Saver works

Peak Saver is BC Hydro's program for easing demand on the power grid at busy times. During a "peak event", BC Hydro may adjust your thermostats by one to three degrees, for no more than four hours at a time. You keep full control: you can override the change or opt out of any event.

  • You can earn a $50 seasonal reward per household, paid as a credit on your bill after the winter season.
  • If you opt out of more than half of the events in a season, you may lose the reward.
  • People who get the free thermostats don't receive Peak Saver's separate sign-up bonus, but they can still earn the seasonal reward.

How to order and install

  1. Check your wiring. Mysa thermostats work with four-wire setups only; Sinopé makes models for two-wire and four-wire setups. Both makers have compatibility checkers on their order pages.
  2. Order through the links on BC Hydro's free smart thermostats page and enrol in Peak Saver at checkout. Shipping is free.
  3. Install them yourself using the makers' video guides, or hire a certified electrician. BC Hydro links to directories of electrical contractors if you're unsure which model fits your home.

BC Hydro warns that high demand is causing shipping delays. You can still order, and you can still earn the reward once your thermostats are installed. BC Hydro's page doesn't list an end date for the offer, so if you want them in place for the coldest months, order soon.

Another way to earn a bill credit this winter

BC Hydro's Team Power Smart challenge pays $100 (or $150 for income-qualified customers) if you cut your home's electricity use by 10% over 12 months. You can start a challenge any time between October 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. Smart thermostats could help you get there.

What it means for landlords

Landlords with baseboard-heated rental units may want to suggest the offer to tenants, since the account holder at the address is the one who orders and enrols. If a tenant asks permission to swap the thermostats, agree in writing on what happens to the old ones and who looks after the new ones when the tenancy ends.

What to do now

  • Confirm your home has electric baseboard heat and check which thermostat model suits your wiring.
  • Sign in to (or set up) your MyHydro account, then order through BC Hydro's free smart thermostats page.
  • Renting? Get your landlord's okay before you order.
  • Keep your order confirmation and note when you installed the thermostats.
  • Shopping for appliances too? See our article on BC Hydro's ENERGY STAR appliance discounts. Replacing your heating instead? Read about the National Heat Pump Rebate.
