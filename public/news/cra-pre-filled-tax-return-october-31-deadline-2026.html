CRA's new pre-filled tax return: set up your account by October 31

By Calcitax Advisors · October 31, 2026

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is launching a free service that fills in your tax return for you using information it already has. Starting in March 2027, about one million Canadians will be invited to use it for their 2026 return. To be considered, you need to have your CRA account set up by October 31, 2026.

What is a pre-filled return?

The CRA already receives most of the slips that simple returns need, such as your T4 from your employer or your pension slips. With the new service, the CRA puts that information into a return for you. You sign in, check everything, add anything that's missing, and then accept and submit it.

It isn't automatic filing. Nothing is filed until you review and submit the return yourself, and you stay responsible for making sure it's correct.

Who may be invited

The first round is aimed at people with lower incomes and simple tax situations who don't owe tax. According to the CRA, you must meet all of these conditions:

  • You have a CRA account, and your correspondence preference is set to electronic mail.
  • You filed your 2025 tax return.
  • You were a Canadian resident for tax purposes for all of 2026, you're filing for yourself, and you were 15 or older in 2026.
  • Your income comes only from certain slips, such as T4, T4A, T4A(OAS), T4A(P), T4E, T5 and T5007, and is below a limit that depends on your province, age and disability tax credit status.

You won't qualify if, in 2026, you sold your principal residence, went bankrupt, repaid COVID-19 benefits or amounts under the Home Buyers' Plan or Lifelong Learning Plan, had exempt income under the Indian Act, or owned specified foreign property costing more than $100,000.

Meeting the conditions doesn't guarantee an invitation. The CRA plans to invite about one million people in 2027 and expand to about 5.5 million by 2029.

What to do by October 31

  1. Sign in to your CRA account, or register if you don't have one.
  2. Check your personal information, including your address and email.
  3. Set your correspondence preference to electronic mail in your account settings.
  4. File your 2025 return if you haven't already. Free options include the CRA's SimpleFile service and community volunteer tax clinics.

If you're invited, you'll get an email in March 2027 telling you the invitation is waiting in your CRA account.

Why filing matters even if you owe nothing

Many benefits are only paid if you file a return every year, including the Canada Child Benefit, the GST/HST credit and several BC credits. A pre-filled return makes it easier for people with simple situations to keep those payments coming.

Is it right for you?

If your only income is from a job or a pension and you have no deductions to claim, a pre-filled return could save you time. But it only includes what the CRA already knows. Credits and deductions that depend on your receipts, such as medical expenses, donations, tuition carry-forwards, moving costs or rent, may be missing unless you add them. If your situation is more than simple, or you're not sure what you can claim, it's worth having your return reviewed.
