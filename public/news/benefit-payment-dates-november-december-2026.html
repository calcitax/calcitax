CCB, CPP and OAS payment dates for November and December 2026

By Calcitax Advisors · November 8, 2026

Two more Canada child benefit payments, two more CPP and Old Age Security deposits and two Canada Disability Benefit payments are left in 2026, and the December dates come earlier than usual because of the holidays. Here is the schedule for the rest of the year, taken from the federal government's official payment calendars, plus what to do if a payment doesn't show up.

What happened

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and Service Canada publish their payment dates in advance. With the year winding down, the remaining dates for 2026 are now the ones that matter. December payments are generally earlier in the month than you might expect, so plan holiday spending around the real deposit date, not the usual one.

Payment dates for the rest of 2026

BenefitNovember 2026December 2026
Canada Disability BenefitNovember 19December 17
Canada child benefit (CCB), including the B.C. family benefitNovember 20December 11
Canada Pension Plan (CPP) and Old Age Security (OAS)November 26December 22
Veterans disability pensionNovember 27December 23

The official calendars do not list any Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit payment (the benefit that replaced the GST/HST credit in July 2026) or advanced Canada workers benefit payment in November or December. The last scheduled payments for both were in October 2026, and the government has not yet posted its 2027 dates. If you were expecting one of these in the next few weeks, nothing is missing.

What it means for families in B.C.

If you get the CCB for a child under 18, the B.C. family benefit is added to the same monthly deposit. You don't apply for it separately: once your child is registered for the CCB and you (and your spouse or partner) have filed your tax returns, the CRA works out the amount automatically.

  • For July 2026 to June 2027, the B.C. family benefit is up to $1,750 a year for a first child, $1,100 for a second child and $900 for each additional child, according to the B.C. government. That is about $145.83 a month for a first child.
  • The amount is based on your 2025 adjusted family net income. Families with income below $30,176 receive the maximum; the amount is reduced above that.
  • If you share custody, each parent gets half the amount for that child.

Our guide to the Canada child benefit and B.C. family benefits explains how these amounts are calculated and who qualifies.

What it means for seniors

CPP and OAS are paid on the same day each month. The December deposit lands on December 22, 2026, a few days before Christmas. If you are retired or helping a parent manage their money, our retirement income guide covers how CPP, OAS and RRIF withdrawals are taxed.

If a payment doesn't arrive

Payments can take a few days to reach your account, and cheques take longer than direct deposit. Before you call:

  • Wait. For the CCB, the CRA asks you to wait 5 working days after the payment date. For other programs, the federal calendar suggests waiting 5 to 10 business days.
  • Check your online account. For the CCB and B.C. family benefit, your CRA account shows the status of your payments. For CPP and OAS, check with Service Canada.
  • Check your information is current. A new address, a change in marital status or a child moving out can pause or change payments. If you move out of B.C., tell the CRA promptly so you don't receive B.C. family benefit payments you would later have to pay back.

What to do now

  • Sign up for direct deposit if you still get cheques. Cheques can take longer to arrive than direct deposit, especially with holiday mail.
  • Make sure both you and your spouse or partner have filed your 2025 tax returns. Benefits like the CCB and B.C. family benefit stop or shrink when a return is missing.
  • Mark the early December dates in your calendar: December 11 for the CCB and December 22 for CPP and OAS.
  • Keep an eye on 2027. Budget 2026 proposed a one-time extra grocery benefit payment in early 2027; our Budget 2026 article explains it. We will share the 2027 payment calendar once the government publishes it.
