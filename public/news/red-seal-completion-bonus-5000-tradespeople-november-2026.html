New $5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus: who qualifies and how to apply

By Calcitax Advisors · November 23, 2026

If you finished an apprenticeship and earned your certification in a Red Seal trade on or after April 1, 2025, you can now apply for a new federal $5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus. Applications opened on November 16, 2026, and the bonus is paid for each eligible trade you certify in.

What happened

The bonus is part of the federal government's Team Canada Strong plan to train and hire more skilled trades workers. Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC), the department that runs Service Canada, announced in October that the online application would open on November 16. The program's pages on canada.ca now describe it as open for continuous intake, which means you can apply at any time within your deadline rather than in a short window.

A Red Seal trade is a trade that meets national standards under the Interprovincial Standards Red Seal Program. You can check whether your trade is on the list at red-seal.ca. A journeyperson is someone who holds a Certificate of Qualification, or its equivalent, from a provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority.

Who qualifies

According to the program's eligibility page, you must:

  • be a Canadian citizen, a permanent resident or a protected person;
  • have completed an apprenticeship as a registered apprentice in a designated Red Seal trade and be a certified journeyperson in that trade;
  • not be a regular full-time high school student;
  • apply within your deadline and send the supporting documents your province requires.

You can receive one bonus for each eligible trade. So if you certify in two Red Seal trades, you would apply separately for each one.

Key dates

  • Certified between April 1, 2025 and November 15, 2026: apply by May 31, 2027.
  • Certified on or after November 16, 2026: apply within 12 months of your certification date.
  • Your supporting documents must arrive within 28 calendar days of the date you apply. If they arrive later, you have to start a new application.

Late applications are not considered, so don't leave this until the last week.

How to apply

Most people will apply through the online application linked from the program's "Apply" page on canada.ca, which ESDC says takes about 15 minutes. You can also call 1-866-742-3644 to ask for a paper form and mail it or drop it off at a Service Canada Centre.

Have these ready before you start:

  • your Social Insurance Number (SIN);
  • your mailing address and email address;
  • your banking details for direct deposit (institution, transit and account numbers);
  • your certification date, the province of certification, your apprentice identification number and the name of your Red Seal trade.

B.C. documents: if you certified in British Columbia, you'll need your Certificate of Apprenticeship and your Certificate of Qualification. Welder A and B also need the Confirmation of Apprenticeship Completion letter. If your SIN starts with 9, you will also need proof of your status in Canada, such as your permanent resident or protected person documents. ESDC says the document list may change, so check it just before you apply.

What happens after you apply

ESDC aims to make an eligibility decision within 28 calendar days of receiving a complete application. Payment is by direct deposit or cheque, and direct deposit is recommended. MoneySense reported that ESDC expects payments to start in December 2026; the program's own pages don't give a start date yet.

The tax side

  • The bonus is taxable. ESDC deducts federal and provincial tax before paying it, so the amount you receive will be less than $5,000.
  • You'll get a T4A slip in the year after you're paid. Include it on that year's tax return. Keep the slip with your tax records.
  • The program's pages say the bonus will not reduce your Employment Insurance (EI) benefits.
  • Because it counts as income, it can affect income-tested benefits and credits, such as the Canada child benefit, a point ESDC also made to MoneySense. If you're near an income cut-off, plan for that.

ESDC also told MoneySense that the bonus will not affect access to other federal apprenticeship supports. The program pages themselves don't address stacking, so we'd treat that as guidance rather than a published rule.

What to do now

  • Check that your trade is a Red Seal trade and find your certification date.
  • Gather your B.C. certificates and SIN before you open the application, so you can send documents well within the 28 days.
  • Choose direct deposit and keep your confirmation emails.
  • Newcomers who are permanent residents or protected persons can qualify too. Our newcomers tax guide explains how slips like the T4A fit into your return.

If you run a trades business, the same Team Canada Strong plan also announced incentives of up to $10,000 for employers who hire a first-year apprentice in an eligible Red Seal trade. We'll cover the details once the application process is published.
