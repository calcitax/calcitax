BC Hydro appliance discounts: up to $200 off ENERGY STAR laundry and fridges

By Calcitax Advisors · November 18, 2026

If a new washer, dryer or fridge is on your holiday shopping list, BC Hydro is offering discounts of $50 to $200 on select ENERGY STAR models bought by January 8, 2027. Most people get the money off at the till, and if you live in certain neighbourhoods, including Campbell Heights in Surrey and White Rock, you may be able to claim a second, matching amount.

What happened

BC Hydro's fall 2026 retail discount program started on October 2, 2026. ENERGY STAR is a certification for appliances that use less energy than standard models, and only listed ENERGY STAR models qualify. Here is what you can save on each eligible appliance:

ApplianceDiscount
Clothes washer$50
Refrigerator$75
Combination washer-dryer$100
Clothes dryer$150
Laundry centre (stacked washer and dryer)$200
Air fryer (select models, instant discount only)$25

Who qualifies

  • You need a mailing address in BC Hydro's service area. Residents of New Westminster can also take part.
  • Only the specific models on BC Hydro's eligible product lists count, so check the model number before you buy.
  • You can claim discounts on a maximum of two appliances during the offer (air fryers have separate limits).
  • Each appliance gets one discount: either the instant discount at the store or a post-purchase claim, never both.

Key dates

  • October 2, 2026: the offer started.
  • January 8, 2027 (11:59 p.m.): last day to order, buy and fully pay for an eligible washer, dryer, fridge, laundry centre or combination unit.
  • March 5, 2027: last day for the air fryer discount.
  • March 8, 2027: deadline to send in a post-purchase application.

BC Hydro can end the offer early if funding runs out or products sell out, so don't leave it to the last week.

How to claim

At the store (instant discount). At participating retailers, the discount comes off automatically at checkout, with no form to fill in. For appliances, the in-store list includes Best Buy, Canadian Appliance Source, Midland Appliance, RONA, The Brick, The Home Depot, Trail Appliances and Visions Electronics. Some of these also offer it online, as long as the delivery postal code is in BC Hydro's area. Not every location or website takes part, so ask before you pay. The discount can't be added after the sale.

After you buy (post-purchase claim). If you bought an eligible appliance where no instant discount was given, you can apply online through BC Hydro's rebate application. You'll need a receipt that shows the model, purchase date, store and price, and that no instant discount was applied. For online orders, keep proof of delivery too. Approved amounts appear as a credit on your BC Hydro bill, which BC Hydro says usually takes one to two billing periods. Your account must be in good standing. Air fryers can't be claimed this way.

Extra money in some neighbourhoods

BC Hydro also runs community-based offers. In select postal codes, the top-up equals 100% of the regular discount, which in effect doubles it. Listed areas include Campbell Heights (Surrey), White Rock, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Mount Lehman, the Mission-to-Agassiz area, Port Coquitlam's Burke Mountain, and some Vancouver neighbourhoods west of Cambie, among others outside the Lower Mainland.

You claim the top-up separately, through a post-purchase application. It depends on funding and approval and doesn't count toward the two-appliance limit. To see whether your address qualifies, enter your postal code on BC Hydro's community-based offers page.

What it means for landlords

If you're buying an appliance for a rental unit, the tax treatment is different from a purchase for your own home. The CRA's capital cost allowance rules say that when you receive a government grant, subsidy or rebate for depreciable property, you generally subtract it from the property's cost before you claim depreciation. In some cases the CRA lets you report government assistance as rental income instead. Keep the receipt and a note of the discount with your rental records. Our rental income tax guide explains how capital cost allowance works.

What to do now

  • Check BC Hydro's eligible model lists before you shop, and confirm the store applies the instant discount.
  • Enter your postal code on the community-based offers page to see whether a top-up applies to you.
  • Keep your receipt, and for online orders, proof of delivery.
  • Buy and pay in full by January 8, 2027, and send any post-purchase claim by March 8, 2027.
  • Replacing your heating instead? Read about the new National Heat Pump Rebate.
