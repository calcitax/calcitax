T5 slips (investment income)

Dividends & interest paid: T5 client form

If your corporation paid dividends to shareholders, or paid interest to anyone, it must give each person a T5 slip and file a T5 Summary with the CRA. For 2026 payments, that's due by March 1, 2027. Tell us who was paid and how much, and we'll prepare and file everything.

For your security, this form asks for only the last 3 digits of each recipient's Social Insurance Number. We'll collect full SINs by phone or in person before filing.
